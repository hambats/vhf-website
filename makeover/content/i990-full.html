A quick guide

Reading a 990

Most people start with Part I, the one-page summary on the first page.

What you'll find inside

  • Revenue for the year, including contributions and grants
  • Expenses, split into program services, management, and fundraising
  • Total assets, liabilities, and net assets
  • Our mission and the programs we ran
  • Who serves on our board and how the organization is governed
