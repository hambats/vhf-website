The program

About the Garden Group

The Garden Group is one community of volunteers working across the vegetable garden (including our heirloom tomatoes) and the flower gardens. No experience is needed, and everyone learns as they go. Rain or shine, the group plants, tends, and harvests every row on the farm, growing the produce behind every free box we give back to the community.

Garden Group sessions are led by Maddie McCann, our Director of Agriculture.

Vegetables

Learn organic farming

Work the soil, pull weeds, plant starts, run the irrigation pumps, tend the greenhouse, cut grass, and pick produce, including our heirloom tomatoes.

  • No experience needed
  • Outdoors

Flowers

Flower design with Sam Raph

Plant, harvest, and care for our flower gardens. The flowers become bouquets we give out at the VA. Sam also teaches free flower design workshops for members each summer.

  • No experience needed
  • Free workshops

Interested? Email veteranshealingfarm@gmail.com

Giving back

Where the harvest goes

Everything grown at the farm is donated back to our veteran community. We regularly distribute produce to these organizations and others in need.

  • Charles George VA Medical Center
  • Veterans Restoration Quarters
  • IAM Food Pantry
  • Hendersonville Rescue Mission
  • The Storehouse
  • SafeLight Women's Shelter
