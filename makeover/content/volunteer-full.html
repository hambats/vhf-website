Volunteer

Get your hands dirty with us

You don't have to be a veteran to volunteer. Our community is veterans, families, caregivers, and anyone who just wants to help, and we'd love to have you.

Why volunteer

What it's like to volunteer here

Come when you can, help with what interests you, and learn as you go.

No required time

There's no set commitment of time or duties. Come and go as you please, and pitch in where you'd like.

No experience needed

Every squad teaches as you go. You'll learn organic farming, flower design, herbs, bees, and more.

You're truly needed

We have a very small staff, so most grants and donations go straight into the farm and events. Volunteers make the rest happen.

The squads

Find your squad

Pick the work that suits you best. You can join more than one.

Garden Group

With Maddie McCann, Director of Agriculture

Grow vegetables and flowers: work the soil, pull weeds, plant starts, run the irrigation pumps, tend the greenhouse, and pick produce, including our heirloom tomatoes. The flowers become bouquets we give out at the VA, and Sam Raph teaches free flower design workshops each summer.

  • No experience needed
  • Outdoors

Meet the Garden Group →

Interested? Email veteranshealingfarm@gmail.com

Medicinal Herb Squad

Led by Rebecca Vann, Plant Medicine Director

Grow local medicinal herbs like echinacea, valerian, skullcap, lemon balm, ginseng, goldenseal, black cohosh, and chamomile. Then help turn them into tinctures, teas, balms, and salves for our veterans and volunteers. Rebecca also teaches free summer workshops for members.

  • No experience needed
  • Free workshops

Interested? Email info@aurumapotheca.com

Bee Squad

Work with our Lead Beekeeper

Raise and care for our honeybees: build and clean hives, bring in wild swarms, split colonies, collect honey, and keep the hives healthy.

  • No experience needed
  • Outdoors

Interested? Email veteranshealingfarm@gmail.com and we'll connect you with our Lead Beekeeper.

How to join

How to get started

Three steps, and then we'll see you at the farm.

  1. Fill out the form

    Start with our short volunteer form.

  2. Pick a squad

    Email the contact for the squad that interests you, or tell us on the form.

  3. Come out to the farm

    Stop by during farm hours. No set schedule; come when you can.
