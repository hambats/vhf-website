Grow together

Agritherapy

Three volunteer groups manage our produce and flower gardens, beehives, and medicinal plants. The healing comes from working together in the soil.

Garden Group

We grow produce and flower bouquets that are donated to veterans and caregivers at the Charles George VA Medical Center, SafeLight Women's Shelter, IAM Food Pantry, Hendersonville Rescue Mission, the Storehouse, and the Veterans Restoration Quarters, a shelter for veterans experiencing homelessness in Asheville. Because the healing comes from the process of working together in the soil, everything we grow is a byproduct to be shared with our veteran community.

Bee Squad

Veteran beekeeping is more than just harvesting honey. It's a powerful form of healing and connection. Working with bees teaches patience, focus, and calm, offering veterans a peaceful, purposeful activity that supports both mental health and the environment. From building hives to tending colonies, veterans gain hands-on skills while helping local ecosystems. Plus, the honey's pretty sweet too!

Medicinal Herb Squad

The Medicinal Herb Squad grows 50 medicinal plants and crafts them into teas, tinctures, salves, and balms, made specifically for our veteran community. For many veterans, the top challenges are sleep, anxiety, and inflammation. The goal of our plant medicine program is to offer natural, plant-based relief instead of just another pill.
