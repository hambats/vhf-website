Where it goes

What your gift supports

While cultivating life through community, we work to bring an end to the alarming suicide rate among our nation's veterans.

Agritherapy

Organic farming, beekeeping, and medicinal herbs: hands-in-the-soil work that helps veterans heal.

Workshops

Therapies and therapeutic workshops, from flower design to herbal medicine, free to our members.

Events

The Veterans Resource Fair, the Traveling Vietnam Memorial Wall, our 9/11 Memorial, Tomatopalooza WNC, live music, and wellness retreats.

“They give away thousands of pounds of produce and hundreds of bouquets of flowers to veterans every year; and they provide a family to many veterans.”

— Don Blackwell, Raven's Wing Studios, East Flat Rock, NC
