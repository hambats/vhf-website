Regular gatherings at the farm

Recurring groups at the farm, 138 Kimzey Road, Mills River, NC. Times are from our public calendar. Groups can pause for weather, holidays, or the off season, so check the calendar above or call (828) 595-6111 before you come.

  • Garden Group with Maddie

    Weekly on Wednesdays, 9am to noon, in the vegetable garden. About the Garden Group

  • Herb Volunteer Group with Rebecca

    Weekly on Fridays, 10am to 2pm, with our Plant Medicine Director. About the Medicinal Herb squad

  • Bee Squad

    Led by our Lead Beekeeper, Michael Morano. Our calendar lists no standing weekly time, so watch it for beekeeping classes and workdays, or ask us. About the Bee Squad

  • Open pottery studio with Sophia

    Open studio hours for past pottery students, offered in seasonal sessions, most recently Saturday mornings (10am to noon) and Wednesday afternoons (1 to 3pm). New students can join a pottery class first. See the calendar for the current session.

  • 2026 Veterans Day 5K

    Sunday, November 8, 2026 at Mills River Park, 124 Town Center Dr., Mills River. Fun Run loop at 9:45am, 5K at 10am. A fundraiser for the farm. Register on Race Roster
