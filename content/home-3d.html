Our approach

How healing happens here

We create a safe, peaceful environment that fosters connection and growth. Our farm-based therapies promote emotional, mental, and physical well-being for veterans and their families.

Everything we grow is given back to our veteran community and other local organizations in need.

  • Organic farming
  • Beekeeping
  • Medicinal herb gardening
  • Therapeutic workshops & activities
  • Community & camaraderie
