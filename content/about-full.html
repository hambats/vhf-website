Our mission & farm

A place to grow, heal, and belong

Since 2013, Veterans Healing Farm in Mills River, NC has offered veterans and their families a safe, peaceful place to heal through the land and each other.

Our mission

To enhance the mental, emotional, and physical well-being of our nation's veterans and their families.

Our vision

An end to the veteran suicide crisis that continues across the country.

Established 2013

Our story

John and Nicole Mahshie founded Veterans Healing Farm in 2013. Today our focus is on improving veteran mental health.

We do that by creating a safe, peaceful environment that fosters healing and camaraderie. Our programs center on brain health.

  • AgritherapyOrganic farming, beekeeping, and medicinal herbs
  • TherapiesTherapeutic activities for body and mind
  • WorkshopsHands-on learning on the farm
  • Live eventsGatherings that build community

“A place where hands find purpose in the earth, where stories are shared without judgment, and where the quiet strength of community helps heal wounds both seen and unseen.”

— Alan J. Yeck, Executive Director, 2025 Year-End Report

Giving back

Everything we grow goes back

Everything grown at the farm is donated back to our veteran community. We regularly distribute produce to these organizations and others in need.

  • Charles George VA Medical Center
  • Veterans Restoration Quarters
  • IAM Food Pantry
  • Hendersonville Rescue Mission
  • The Storehouse
  • SafeLight Women's Shelter / Dandelion Café
  • Carolina Reserve of Laurel Park and Hendersonville
  • The Landing
