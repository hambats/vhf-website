Our board

Board of Directors

Veterans Healing Farm in Mills River, North Carolina, is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. A volunteer board of veterans and community professionals guides our mission, our finances and our future.

Directors

Meet our directors

Every director volunteers their time, so the farm can stay open at no cost to veterans and their families.

  • GJ

    Dr. Gabrielle Juba, CPA, CFE

    President

    U.S. Marine Corps

    Gabi Juba is the CFO of Creating a Family and the founder of Juba Forensics PLLC. She volunteers on the NCACPA’s Young Professional Cabinet and its Education Resource Group. Outside of work, Gabi volunteers with her certified therapy dog, Harley Quinn, and enjoys hiking, gardening, dancing, and competing in triathlons and half marathons.

  • FS

    MSgt. Fran Simone

    Treasurer

    U.S. Air Force, Retired

    Fran Simone is a retired U.S. Air Force Master Sergeant with 21 years of service. In the military and in his professional career, his expertise is the acquisition, development, operations, and sustainment of C4ISR (Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance) systems. Fran enjoys the outdoors, supports local and national organizations for children and families, and advocates for and supports fellow veterans and their families.

  • RL

    MSgt. Robert Lauterbach

    Director

    U.S. Marine Corps, Retired

    Robert Lauterbach is a retired U.S. Marine Corps Master Sergeant with 20 years of service. As a Communications Chief, he led and managed tactical communications systems and teams. Today he is a network engineer, AI programmer, FPV drone pilot, and local artist.

  • JD

    Dr. Jeanine Davis

    Director

    Jeanine Davis is an associate professor and extension specialist in the Department of Horticultural Science at NC State University. She is based at the Mountain Horticultural Crops Research and Extension Center in Mills River.

  • RK

    Rob Kinard

    Director

    U.S. Army, Retired

    Rob Kinard is a veteran with 27 years of service, most of it in U.S. Army Special Forces as both an 18C (Special Forces Engineer) and an 18F (Special Forces Intelligence Sergeant). He deployed to Afghanistan and Iraq 19 times, as a Green Beret and as a contractor supporting the U.S. Government in different capacities. Rob owns Good Times Mead and Good Times Farm and runs Good Times Tactical, which supports U.S. Special Operations Forces with training courses and communications systems. Stopping veteran suicide is at the top of his priorities, and the farm’s charter is in perfect alignment with his own.

  • SW

    Col. Steve Watkins

    Director

    U.S. Army, Retired

    Steve Watkins is a retired U.S. Army Colonel and aviator with more than 31 years of service. He now champions veterans’ initiatives, serves on several community organization boards, and supports healing and connection through community service.

  • DH

    CPO Delmar Hobkirk

    Director

    U.S. Navy, Retired

    Delmar Hobkirk brings two decades of military leadership and operational expertise to the board. In his 20-year Navy career, he served aboard two ships, completed key assignments within the Navy Expeditionary Combat Command, and spent three tours instructing and mentoring the next generation of service members. Since retiring from the Navy in 2018, he has worked in the construction industry, the last five and a half years of it in Western North Carolina. Delmar and his wife are active foster parents and enjoy traveling together.
