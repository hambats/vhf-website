Grow together

Agritherapy: gardens, bees, and herbs

Agritherapy is farm therapy for veterans: real work outdoors, done together. Three volunteer groups tend our vegetable and flower gardens, our beehives, and our medicinal herbs.

Garden Group

Grow vegetables and flowers with Maddie McCann, our Director of Agriculture, then give them away. Produce and bouquets go to veterans and caregivers at the Charles George VA Medical Center, Veterans Restoration Quarters (an Asheville shelter for veterans experiencing homelessness), IAM Food Pantry, Hendersonville Rescue Mission, The Storehouse, SafeLight Women's Shelter and its Dandelion Café, Carolina Reserve of Laurel Park and Hendersonville, and The Landing. The harvest is a bonus. The healing happens in the work itself, side by side in the soil.

Bee Keeping Squad

Work the hives alongside our Lead Beekeeper, Michael Morano. Beekeeping asks for patience, focus, and calm, and it gives back a quiet sense of purpose. You'll build hives, tend the colonies, and help the local ecosystem along the way. And yes, there's honey: 15 gallons in 2026.

Medicinal Herb Squad

Grow 46 medicinal plants with Rebecca Vann, our Director of Plant Medicine, and turn them into teas, tinctures, salves, and balms for the veteran community. For many veterans, the top challenges are sleep, anxiety, and inflammation. The goal is natural, plant-based relief, not just another pill.
